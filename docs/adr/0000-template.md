# ADR-NNNN: Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Sources:** commit hashes, files, or the discussion that led here

## Context

What problem or alternative existed. Two to five sentences.

## Decision

What was decided, as bullets where there is more than one point.

## Consequences

What follows from it: costs, invariants other code must respect, what
became possible or impossible.

## Rejected alternatives

Optional. What was considered and why it lost, with a date if it was
decided separately from the main decision.
